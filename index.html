About Adichunchanagiri University

A spiritual legacy. A scholarly mission.

Adichunchanagiri University (ACU) was founded to blend spiritual wisdom with scientific inquiry, drawing from the 1800-year-old legacy of the Sri Adichunchanagiri Mahasamsthana Math.

Established under Jagadguru Sri Sri Sri Dr. Balagangadharanatha Mahaswamiji, and now led by Jagadguru Sri Sri Sri Dr. Nirmalanandanatha Mahaswamiji (Chancellor, ACU), the university believes education should empower individuals, uplift communities, and serve humanity.

ACU Online, the university's digital learning platform, brings the same academic excellence, ethical grounding and societal vision as its campus programmes to first-generation graduates, working professionals and lifelong learners across the globe.

Programme Overview

MBA (Online Mode)

A two-year, four-semester postgraduate programme designed to develop managerial competence, analytical ability, ethical leadership and industry readiness. It integrates core management disciplines, technology-enabled business practices, research orientation and strategic decision-making for dynamic global and Indian business environments.

  • NEP-aligned and UGC-compliant curriculum suitable for online delivery
  • Strong foundation courses in economics, accounting, statistics, ethics and communication
  • Functional specialisation exposure in marketing, finance, HR, operations, banking and analytics
  • Technology and analytics orientation through Emerging Technologies and Business Analytics
  • Research-driven approach with Business Research Methods and a capstone project
  • Flexible online learning model, ideal for working professionals and fresh graduates
  • Industry-relevant project work worth 12 credits in the final semester
